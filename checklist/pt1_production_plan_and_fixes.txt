================================================================================
1. RECENT FIXES COMPLETED IN ASSIGNMENT 4 ALPHA BUILD
================================================================================
- Reverse-to-Forward Math/NaN Fix:
  Resolved runtime error during transition from reverse (speed < 0) to forward (W key). Guarded power law calculations against fractional powers of negative numbers (Math.pow(normRatio, 1.15)) and clamped acceleration values to guarantee zero NaN propagation.

- Manual Steering Direction Correction:
  Flipped manual steering input mapping so pressing 'A' (or Left Arrow) correctly turns the vehicle left (counter-clockwise yaw), and pressing 'D' (or Right Arrow) turns the vehicle right (clockwise yaw). Synchronized 3D front wheel steer assemblies and steering wheel mesh rotations accordingly.

- Dynamic Environmental Asset Scaling:
  Implemented updateEnvironmentForTrack(boundingRadius, trackLength). Outer pine forest belts, distant mountain rings, and 16 safe infield groves now dynamically reposition, re-scale, and maintain strict road clearances (minimum 22m from road edge) whenever track length (1,000m to 10,000m), control handles, or F1 presets change.

- Screen Flickering & Tearing Elimination:
  Removed redundant secondary interval timer (setInterval(tick, 11)) that caused double-render buffer thrashing alongside requestAnimationFrame. Added logarithmicDepthBuffer support with safe fallback, adjusted camera near plane to 0.6m, and enforced negative polygon offsets on road asphalt, curbs, and finish lines to permanently resolve z-fighting.

- Trackside Audience & Safety Clearance:
  Grandstands and elevated viewing berms are dynamically calculated with a guaranteed 6.0m buffer outside road curbs so spectators never clip through the racing surface across all track layouts.
