1. EXECUTIVE SPRINT SUMMARY: ALPHA TO BETA TRANSITION
================================================================================
In Assignment 4 (Alpha Build), we established the foundational 3-state game loop (Start lights countdown, active 90 FPS racing, and checkered flag finish celebration), verified 5 official Grand Prix circuits (Monza, Silverstone, Spa, Monaco, Suzuka), and unified manual WASD control with heuristic autonomous AI navigation.

In Assignment 5 (Beta Build), our development focus expanded our vertical slice by directly connecting secondary technical pillars into the active driving loop:
- Hardware-instanced GPU particle system for tire smoke and skidmark decals.
- Thermal friction brake disc incandescence shader (molten orange-red glow under threshold braking, convective air cooling at speed).
- Multi-car racing simulation featuring an AI sparring opponent (Ferrari Scarlet livery) with dynamic lookahead pacing and real-time HUD gap tracking.
- Curvilinear boundary collision with curb rumble haptics (chassis vertical shudder, camera screen shake, and 48 Hz Web Audio vibration).
- Live 90 Hz multi-channel telemetry canvas waveform oscillogram plotting speed, throttle, brake, and lateral G traces.
- Procedural atmospheric sky gradient texture enhancing overall visual cohesion.
